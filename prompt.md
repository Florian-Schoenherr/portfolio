c. Focus on the "STAR" Method for Case Studies
Since they are IT students, they might get bogged down in the "how" (the code). Encourage them to use the
STAR method to keep it business-friendly:
• Situation: What was the business problem?
• Task: What was the specific goal?
• Action: What technical and PM steps did they take?
• Result: What was the outcome? (e.g., "Reduced load time by 20%" or "Automated a 5-hour manual
process").
TIP: A business portfolio is a marketing tool, not a technical manual. It’s about convincing
a stakeholder that they are organized, communicative, and capable of solving problems
