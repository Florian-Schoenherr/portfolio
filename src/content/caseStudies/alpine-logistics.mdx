import StarSection from '../../components/StarSection.astro';

<StarSection label="Situation">
  Alpine Logistics' dispatch team started every day by hand-copying the
  previous night's shipment plan into a 14-tab Excel workbook, cross-checking
  driver availability, and manually assigning routes. The ritual took five
  hours. A single mistyped cell could cascade into missed SLAs across the
  eastern region, and the institutional knowledge lived in the head of one
  senior dispatcher who was weeks away from retiring.
</StarSection>

<StarSection label="Task">
  Reduce daily dispatch preparation to under an hour, remove the single-person
  bottleneck, and make the routing logic inspectable and auditable — without
  replacing the ERP or retraining the drivers. The engagement had a 10-week
  budget and a hard go-live before the pre-Christmas freight peak.
</StarSection>

<StarSection label="Action">
  - Ran a two-week process mining sprint to reconstruct the dispatcher's
    implicit rules as an explicit decision model.
  - Built a Python + FastAPI service that reads the overnight shipment plan
    from the ERP, applies the decision model, and writes a proposed daily
    route set into a new PostgreSQL table.
  - Wrapped the job in Apache Airflow with idempotent retries and a full
    audit trail.
  - Delivered a thin review UI so dispatchers can approve, tweak, or override
    any route before the driver apps pick it up.
  - Stood up Grafana dashboards for SLA adherence and route-override rate,
    so the team could tell us the day the model needed retraining.
  - Shadowed the senior dispatcher during three parallel runs before cut-over.
</StarSection>

<StarSection label="Result">
  Dispatch preparation dropped from **5 hours to ~20 minutes** of review.
  SLA adherence rose from 91% to **98%** in the first quarter after go-live,
  and manual dispatcher errors fell by **86%**. The retiring dispatcher's
  knowledge is now encoded, versioned, and reviewable — and the team has
  added two more automated rules on their own since handover.
</StarSection>
