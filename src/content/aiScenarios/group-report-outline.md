title: "The group report with an AI outline"
context: Business Informatics · Group coursework
classification: yellow
order: 1
---

A three-person project group used a generative AI assistant to produce
a first-pass outline and to rephrase sentences they found awkward in
their draft report. They wrote the analysis and conclusions themselves,
but several paragraphs still read like lightly edited model output.
The module handbook requires work to be **"independent and your own"**
but does not mention generative AI, spell-checkers, or translation
tools. The group submitted without a declaration and without citing
the assistant anywhere.
