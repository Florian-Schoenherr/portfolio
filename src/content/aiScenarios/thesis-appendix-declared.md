For a master's thesis on process automation, the author used a
generative AI tool only to **proofread grammar** and to suggest clearer
wording for two figure captions. Every change was reviewed manually.
The thesis includes a short subsection describing which sections were AI-assisted and how
outputs were verified before inclusion.
