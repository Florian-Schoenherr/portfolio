context: Software Engineering · Individual assessment

During a 90-minute online exam, a student kept a browser tab with a
generative AI chatbot open beside the proctoring window. For each
open-ended question they pasted the prompt, copied the answer, and
submitted it under their own name. The student did not declare the tool anywhere on the
submission.
